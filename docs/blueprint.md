# **App Name**: telormulu

## Core Features:

- Pecut AI Recipe Generator: An AI-powered recipe generator that uses the ingredients in your pantry to create a creative and humorous tool-driven tutorial tailored for students.
- Anti-Sultan Local Guardrail: Validation logic that intercepts ingredients like 'wagyu' or 'caviar' and triggers a witty Indonesian slang alert for users with high specs.
- Interactive Pantry Interface: Dynamic controls including an instant egg counter incrementer and common boarding house ingredient checkboxes.
- Community Recipe Grid: A curated grid display of budget-friendly static recipes with interactive state-based navigation to detail views.
- Budget Cooking Dashboard: Single-page application flow using Next.js state management for seamless transitions between home and manual cooking modes.
- Integrated Video Tutorial Player: A focused detail page featuring markdown steps and embedded video players mapped to specific recipe contexts.

## Style Guidelines:

- The palette is centered on the 'telur' theme. Primary color: Deep Yolk Amber (#D97706) for text and borders to ensure readability. Background: Soft Milk White (#FAFAF9) to keep the app feeling airy and minimal.
- Accent color: Bright Egg Yolk (#FBBF24), used exclusively for high-priority action buttons and active UI states for high visibility.
- One font recommendation: 'Poppins' for a precise, geometric, yet friendly sans-serif look that aligns with a contemporary Indonesian college aesthetic.
- Custom outline icons based on common 'kos-kosan' items (frying pan, rice cooker, and iron), maintaining the #D97706 stroke weight.
- A structured vertical-first layout with high-contrast card containers (#FFFFFF) featuring thin borders and clear hierarchy for instructions.
- Subtle slide transitions between the navigation states to maintain the Single-Page Application (SPA) feel during the cooking process.
